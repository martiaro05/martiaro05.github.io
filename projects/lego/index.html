Lego Mindstorms EV3

2025 • Python

Beginning my first semester at NTNU, we have worked with Lego mindstorms EV3 in python.

This is a team-based project carried out with Team 13, focused on building and programming LEGO Mindstorms robots.

So far, we have completed three test projects to experiment with different mechanics and programming techniques. In addition, we successfully finished an IDI race, putting our skills to the test in a competitive environment.

Currently, we are starting a new custom LEGO project, where we aim to design and build a unique robot tailored to specific challenges and tasks. This project will combine teamwork, problem-solving, and creative engineering.
