Lego mindstorms EV3 project

2025 • Python

Beginning my first semester at NTNU, we have worked with Lego mindstorms EV3 in python.

This is a team-based project carried out with Team 13, focused on building and programming LEGO Mindstorms robots.

So far, we have completed three test projects to experiment with different mechanics and programming techniques. In addition, we successfully finished an IDI rally race, putting our skills to the test in a competitive environment.

Flora servus 3000

As a final projects for this class, we developed a gardener robot to help streamline the agriculture industry. Our goal was to design and build a unique robot tailored to work in a small garden, and large fields.

I was assigned a project coordinator role, to keep structure of the progress being made, and help out the other teammembers on their assigned field. The robot did well during demonstration, and resulted in being a successfull prototype!
